## 0:00 Hook (≤15 sec)
State the payoff or show the result. No intro, no logo.

## 0:15 Setup (≤45 sec)
Why this matters. Where it comes from (credit Snyder and Dayton).

## 1:00 The Rule
Gold-on-black card. Explain it in plain words.

## 2:00 In the gym
Show it in real sets. Voice-over the cues. Show the log book.

## Main body
2–3 sections, each with a mini-hook ("but here's what most people miss...").

## Build update (optional, 30 sec)
Bodyweight, one lift, how training feels.

## Close
One-sentence recap, then point to the next video: "Next week I'm testing ____. Watch this one in the meantime."
