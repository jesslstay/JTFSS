# Content Pillars

Five recurring formats. Each one has a clear job.

| # | Pillar | Job | Length | How often |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Build** (progress series) | Retention: people subscribe to see what happens next | 10–15 min | Every 4 weeks |
| 2 | **Old-School Rules** (teach one principle) | Search traffic and authority | 8–12 min | Weekly |
| 3 | **Full Workout, Train With Me** | Watch time and community | 15–25 min | Every 2 weeks |
| 4 | **The Old-School Kitchen** (diet) | Search traffic plus a broader audience | 8–12 min | Every 2 weeks |
| 5 | **Golden-Era Routines** (champion routines from history, with credit) | Shareable, nostalgic, browse traffic | 10–15 min | Monthly |

Plus **Shorts**: 3–5 per week, cut from the long videos (a tip, a lift, a meal, a "Rule" card).

## Video ideas by pillar

### 1. The Build
- Day 1: where I'm starting (honest measurements and a physique check)
- Week 4 / 8 / 12 check-ins
- "What I'd change": a look back after each 12-week block
- One year of old-school training: side-by-side comparison

### 2. Old-School Rules
- What George Snyder meant by "Freestyle" training *(from the book)*
- "Three More Reps": the old-school approach to intensity
- Why golden-era physiques looked different: proportion and symmetry versus pure size
- Compound lifts first: the basic movements that built the classics
- Supersets the old way
- Train the weak point first
- How to keep a training log like it's 1975
- Posing practice as training: why the old guys flexed between sets
- Rest days and sleep: the old-school take on recovery
- Volume vs. intensity: what the golden era got right and wrong

### 3. Full Workout, Train With Me
- Workout A, B and C from the 12-week program
- Arm day, golden-era style
- 45-minute garage-gym full body session

### 4. The Old-School Kitchen
- What I eat in a day: old-school bulk
- Eggs, milk and beef: the golden-era staples, revisited
- A cheap old-school grocery haul
- Cutting for summer without a single supplement
- Snyder's diet rules, tested for 30 days *(from the book)*

### 5. Golden-Era Routines
- Routines from *3 More Reps*, one champion per video (credit the book, use your own demo footage)
- How the champs trained calves, shoulders, chest...
- I tried a 1970s champion's routine for 4 weeks

## Rules for every video
1. The hook (first 15 seconds) states the payoff or shows the result.
2. One principle per video.
3. Show it in a real set within 2 minutes.
4. Cite the source on screen.
5. End by pointing to the next video, not with "like and subscribe."
