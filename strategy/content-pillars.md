# Content Pillars

Five recurring formats. Each one has a clear job.

| # | Pillar | Job | Length | How often |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Build** (progress series) | Retention: people subscribe to see what happens next | 10–15 min | Every 4 weeks |
| 2 | **Freestyle Rules** (teach one principle from the book) | Search traffic and authority | 8–12 min | Weekly |
| 3 | **Full Workout, Train With Me** | Watch time and community | 20–45 min | Every 2 weeks |
| 4 | **The Old-School Kitchen** (diet) | Search traffic plus a broader audience | 8–12 min | Every 2 weeks |
| 5 | **Myths & History** (women and weights, then and now) | Shareable, comment-driving | 8–12 min | Monthly |
| 6 | **Golden-Era Champions** (a routine or principle from *The Science of Advanced Bodybuilding*, adapted for women) | Nostalgia, browse traffic, advanced viewers | 10–15 min | Monthly, weekly during Weeks 13–24 |

Plus **Shorts**: 3–5 per week, cut from the long videos (a tip, an exercise, a meal, a "Rule" card).

## Video ideas by pillar

### 1. The Build
- Day 1: where I'm starting (honest measurements, no shame)
- Week 4 / 8 / 12 check-ins
- "The scale didn't move, but look at this": measurement and photo videos
- One year of Freestyle Training: side by side

### 2. Freestyle Rules
- What *Free Style Training* actually says (book breakdown)
- Fatigue vs. heavy weight: why the book trains to fatigue
- Tempo and control: making light weights feel heavy
- Supersets and giant sets for shape
- Training the back of the arm the Freestyle way
- Glutes: the Freestyle way vs. the modern way
- How to keep a training log
- Rest, sleep and hormones: recovery for women who lift

### 3. Full Workout, Train With Me
- Workout A, B and C from the 12-week program
- 30-minute dumbbell-only home Freestyle session
- Glute & leg burnout, real time

### 4. The Old-School Kitchen
- What I eat in a day to tone up (with protein)
- High-protein breakfasts without powders
- Budget grocery haul for a lean, strong body
- Losing fat without starving: the old-school way
- The book's diet rules, tested for 30 days *(from the book)*

### 5. Myths & History
- "Will lifting make me bulky?" The honest answer
- Laura Dayton and the women who pioneered bodybuilding
- The first Ms. Olympia and how it changed women's fitness
- 1990s fitness magazines vs. today: what they got right
- Cardio-only vs. weights: what really changes your shape

### 6. Golden-Era Champions
- "Three more reps": the golden-era secret, explained
- Frank Zane's approach to symmetry and a small waist, adapted for women
- I trained like a 1970s champion for 4 weeks (one champion per block)
- How the champions trained shoulders / back / calves / arms
- The nutrition section of *3 More Reps* (Vol. 2): what holds up today
- Freestyle vs. the champions: what women can borrow and what to skip

*Always credit volume and page, and demonstrate the routine yourself instead of showing the book's pages.*

## Rules for every video
1. The hook (first 15 seconds) states the payoff or shows the result.
2. One principle per video.
3. Show it in a real set within 2 minutes.
4. Cite the source on screen.
5. End by pointing to the next video, not with "like and subscribe."
