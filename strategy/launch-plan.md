# 90-Day Launch Plan

## Before upload #1 (week 0)
- [ ] Lock in the name, then claim the @handle on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok
- [ ] Banner, profile icon, About section (`brand/channel-identity.md`)
- [ ] Channel keywords: weight training for women, toning, strength training for women, old school training, free style training, George Snyder, Laura Dayton, women who lift, firm not bulky
- [ ] Film the "Day 1" physique check and measurements (this is the most important footage you'll shoot)
- [ ] Batch-film videos 1–3 before launch so you start with a buffer
- [ ] Gear: phone + tripod, clip-on mic (audio matters more than video), one soft light for indoor shots

## Upload schedule
- **1 long video per week**, same day and time (e.g. Sunday 10am)
- **3–5 Shorts per week**, cut from the long video
- Community post mid-week: poll or log-book photo

## Shorts formula (under 60 seconds)
1. Hook line on screen (e.g. "The 1995 trick for firmer arms")
2. Demo
3. One cue
4. "Full workout on the channel"

## What to measure (YouTube Studio, weekly)
- **Click-through rate:** aim for 4–8%. If it's low, change the thumbnail or title.
- **Average view duration / retention at 30 seconds:** aim for 70%+. If it's low, tighten the hook.
- **Returning viewers:** this is the Build series working.
- Test 2–3 thumbnails per video with YouTube's "Test & Compare."

## Growth habits
- Answer every comment in the first hour after uploading.
- Pin a comment asking a question ("What's your weak point?").
- Use viewer questions as future video topics.
- Collaborate with other women's strength, natural fitness and fitness-history creators after the first 10 videos.

## Monetization path (later)
1. YouTube Partner Program (1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours, or the Shorts route)
2. Printable training log and 12-week program PDF
3. Old-school merch (log books, T-shirts)
4. Coaching, once you have results to show
