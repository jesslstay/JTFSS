# Freestyle Foundation: the 12-Week Program

This is the flagship program the channel follows on camera. It's built on general golden-era principles: heavy compound lifts first, moderate-to-high volume, supersets for antagonist muscles, and special focus on the shoulders, back width and arms for a classic V-taper.

> **Align with Snyder:** once the worksheet in `source-notes/snyder.md` is filled in, adjust the split, rep ranges and intensity methods here to match the book, and say so on camera ("this is the Snyder version").

## Overview
- **Split:** 3 training days on a rotating A/B/C cycle, 4 days a week (e.g. Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri). The cycle continues each week, so Week 1 = A B C A, Week 2 = B C A B...
- **Phases:**
  - **Weeks 1–4: Foundation.** Learn the lifts, find working weights, leave 2–3 reps in the tank.
  - **Weeks 5–8: Build.** Add weight or reps every session. Bring in supersets.
  - **Weeks 9–12: Intensify.** On the last set of each main lift, push to a hard rep max and add "three more reps" with a 10–15 second rest-pause. Week 12 is a lighter deload/test week.
- **Progression:** double progression. When you hit the top of the rep range on every set, add the smallest weight increment next time.
- **Warm-up:** 5 minutes easy cardio, then 2–3 ramp-up sets on the first lift.

## Rest
- Main lifts: 2–3 minutes
- Accessories: 60–90 seconds
- Supersets: no rest between the pair, 90 seconds after

## The old-school habits
- **Keep a written log.** Every set, every rep, every weight. Show it on camera.
- **Flex between sets.** Hold the trained muscle hard for 10–20 seconds (mind-muscle connection and posing practice).
- **Posing practice:** 10 minutes, 2–3 times a week. Front double biceps, side chest, back double biceps, vacuum.
- **Sleep 7–9 hours.** Growth happens out of the gym.
