# Freestyle Foundation: the 12-Week Program

This is the flagship program the channel follows on camera. It's built on the core idea reported for George Snyder and Laura Dayton's *Free Style Training* (1995): **fatigue the muscle to firm and shape it, rather than chasing size.** It mixes that idea with classic old-school weight training habits: free weights, supersets, a written log and steady progression.

> **Align with the book:** once the worksheet in `source-notes/snyder.md` is filled in, swap in the book's exact sets, reps, tempo, exercise order and rest periods, and say so on camera ("this is the Freestyle version, page __").

## How it works
- **Fatigue, not max weight.** Use a load you can control for 12–20 reps and take each set close to fatigue: the last 2–3 reps should burn and slow down.
- **Supersets and giant sets.** Pair exercises back to back to keep the muscle working and the session efficient.
- **Controlled tempo.** 2 seconds up, 2–3 seconds down, a brief squeeze at the top. No swinging.
- **Shape priorities:** glutes and hamstrings, shoulders (cap and posture), back (a narrower-looking waist), arms (back of the arm especially), and core.

## Overview
- **4 days a week** (e.g. Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri), rotating Workouts A, B and C, plus 1–2 walks or light cardio sessions.
- **About 45 minutes** per session.
- **Phases:**
  - **Weeks 1–4: Learn.** 2 sets per exercise, stop 2–3 reps before fatigue. Nail technique and tempo.
  - **Weeks 5–8: Fatigue.** 3 sets, take the last set of each exercise to fatigue. Bring in supersets.
  - **Weeks 9–12: Freestyle finish.** Giant sets and a "burnout" finisher at the end of each workout. Week 12 is lighter, with a progress test.
- **Progression:** when you can do the top of the rep range with good form on every set, add a little weight (the smallest dumbbell jump) or slow the tempo.

## Rest
- Between supersets/giant sets: 60–90 seconds
- Single exercises: 45–60 seconds

## The old-school habits
- **Keep a written log.** Every set, rep and weight. Show it on camera.
- **Mind-muscle connection.** Squeeze the working muscle at the top of every rep.
- **Posture and posing practice** (a few minutes, 2–3 times a week): it shows your shape and builds control.
- **Sleep 7–9 hours.** Firmness comes from recovering, not just training.
