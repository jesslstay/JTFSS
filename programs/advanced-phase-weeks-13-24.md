# Advanced Phase: Weeks 13–24 ("3 More Reps")

This phase follows the 12-week Freestyle Foundation. It adds the advanced layer from George Snyder and Rick Wayne's *3 More Reps! The Science of Advanced Bodybuilding* (1978–79): pushing past fatigue, champion-inspired routines, and a focus on proportion and symmetry.

> **Align with the book:** the structure below is a placeholder built on general advanced-training practice. Replace the intensity method, split and featured routines with what's in the book (`source-notes/science-of-advanced-bodybuilding.md`), and cite volume and page on camera.

## Only start this phase if:
- You've completed the 12-week Foundation (or 3+ months of consistent lifting)
- Your form holds up to the last rep of a fatigued set
- You're recovering well: sleeping, not constantly sore, energy steady

## What changes from the Foundation
| | Foundation (1–12) | Advanced (13–24) |
|---|---|---|
| Days per week | 4 | 4–5 |
| Main idea | Reach fatigue | Reach fatigue, then **3 more reps** |
| Rep range | 12–20 | 8–12 on main lifts, 12–20 on shaping work |
| Split | 3 rotating workouts | Body-part split with a weak-point day |
| New focus | Technique, tempo | Proportion, symmetry, posing |

## "3 More Reps" (default method until the book's version is added)
On the **last set** of each main exercise only:
1. Do as many good reps as you can.
2. Rest 10–15 seconds, holding the weight or racking it.
3. Get **3 more reps**. Rest again if needed, but get all three with good form.

Never do this on squats, deadlifts or anything where a failed rep is dangerous without a spotter. Use machines, dumbbells or a partner.

## Split (5 days; drop Day 5 for a 4-day week)
| Day | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Glutes & hamstrings |
| 2 | Back & rear delts (width for a smaller-looking waist) |
| 3 | Rest or walk |
| 4 | Quads & calves |
| 5 | Shoulders & arms |
| 6 | **Weak point + posing** (your chosen body part, 20 min posing practice) |
| 7 | Rest |
