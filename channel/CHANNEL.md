## Growth playbook (what works for ambient channels)
- **The live stream is the storefront.** It ranks for "fireplace live" and gets TV traffic all evening. Keep it running uninterrupted, because each restart creates a new video and resets watch-time momentum.
- **Upload a 10-hour version weekly** with slight variations (later: different flame color grades, rain + fire, snow outside). Long watch time is what the algorithm rewards in this niche.
- **Seasonality:** traffic peaks October–February and especially around Christmas and New Year's Eve. Schedule holiday titles ("Christmas Fireplace…", "New Year's Eve Fireplace…") in November.
- **TV is the primary screen.** Thumbnails must read at couch distance: big serif number, dark background, warm fire.
- **Community tab:** a simple "Fire's lit 🔥 What are you doing tonight?" post drives return viewers.

## Monetization notes (read before applying to YPP)
- YouTube's *reused / repetitive content* policy can affect looped ambient videos. Everything in this
  channel is **original** (procedurally generated fire, synthesized audio, our own branding), which helps.
  Variety between uploads (new looks, scenes, sound mixes) helps more.
- If you swap in a recorded fire sound, make sure you own it or it's licensed for commercial use
  (e.g. your own recording, or CC0 from Freesound with the license noted).
- YPP thresholds: 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 public watch hours (12 months). Long ambient videos and
  livestreams accumulate watch hours quickly.
