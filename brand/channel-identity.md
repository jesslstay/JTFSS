## Voice on camera
- **Coach in the gym, not a lecturer.** Talk while you train, keep it short, then demonstrate.
- **Honest.** Show real numbers, bad days and plateaus. The progress is the story.
- **Respect the history.** Credit the source every time: "Snyder writes..." or "the golden-era guys did..."
- **No supplement hype, no bashing other creators.**
- Catchphrase options: "Three more reps." / "Earn the shape." / "Back to the iron."

## Visual style
- **Look:** warm film tones, a bit of grain, chalk dust, steel plates, a worn gym. Think a 1970s gym photo, but sharp.
- **Colors:**
  - Iron black `#1B1B1B`
  - Bone white `#F2EBDD`
  - Golden-era gold `#C9962B`
  - Brick red accent `#9E3B2B`
- **Fonts:** a heavy condensed sans for titles (Bebas Neue, Oswald), a slab serif for quotes (Roboto Slab).
- **Thumbnails:** one physique or lift photo, 2–4 words in big type, gold or red highlight, high contrast. Put a small "WEEK X" badge on progress videos.
- **Banner:** 2560×1440 (keep text inside the center 1546×423). Tagline on the left, upload schedule on the right, dark gym photo behind.

## Recurring on-screen elements
- **"The Rule"** card: the principle the video teaches, shown in gold on black.
- **Log book:** film your written training log for each session (sets × reps × weight).
- **Progress tracker:** bodyweight, waist, arm and one strength lift, updated every 4 weeks.
