# The Old-School Diet

Golden-era eating in a nutshell: **lots of protein from whole food, simple meals, consistency, and eat for the goal.** No powders needed (they're fine for convenience).

## Set your numbers
Starting points. Adjust every 2 weeks based on your 7-day average bodyweight.

| Goal | Calories / day | Weight change target |
|---|---|---|
| Lean bulk | bodyweight (lb) × 16–18 | +0.25–0.5% of bodyweight per week |
| Maintain / recomp | bodyweight (lb) × 14–15 | stable |
| Cut | bodyweight (lb) × 11–13 | −0.5–1% of bodyweight per week |

- **Protein:** 0.8–1 g per lb of bodyweight per day
- **Fat:** about 0.3–0.4 g per lb
- **Carbs:** the rest, mostly around training

*Example (180 lb, lean bulk):* ~3,000 kcal → 180 g protein, 65 g fat, ~420 g carbs.

## The staples
- **Protein:** eggs, whole milk, beef (ground and steak), chicken, canned tuna and sardines, cottage cheese, Greek yogurt, liver (once a week, old-school style)
- **Carbs:** oats, potatoes, rice, whole-grain bread, fruit, beans
- **Fats:** butter, olive oil, nuts, peanut butter, cheese
- **Vegetables:** whatever's in season. Every meal.

## Template day: lean bulk (~3,000 kcal)
| Meal | Food |
|---|---|
| Breakfast | 4 eggs, 2 slices toast with butter, oatmeal with banana, coffee |
| Lunch | 8 oz ground beef, 2 cups rice, vegetables |
| Pre-workout | Greek yogurt, honey, fruit |
| Post-workout | Glass of whole milk, tuna sandwich |
| Dinner | 8 oz chicken or steak, large potato, salad with olive oil |
| Before bed | 1 cup cottage cheese, handful of nuts |

## Template day: cut (~2,200 kcal)
| Meal | Food |
|---|---|
| Breakfast | 3 whole eggs + 3 whites, ½ cup oats, berries |
| Lunch | 8 oz chicken, 1 cup rice, big salad |
| Snack | Greek yogurt or cottage cheese |
| Dinner | 8 oz lean beef or fish, vegetables, small potato |
| Optional | Apple and a small handful of nuts |

## Weekly budget grocery list
- 3 dozen eggs
- 1 gallon whole or 2% milk
- 3 lb ground beef, 3 lb chicken thighs or breast
- 4 cans tuna
- 2 tubs cottage cheese or Greek yogurt
- Oats, rice, potatoes, bread
- Bananas, apples, frozen berries
- Frozen vegetables, salad greens
- Peanut butter, olive oil, butter

## Rules
1. Hit protein every day. Everything else is secondary.
2. Weigh yourself each morning and track the 7-day average, not single days.
3. Eat mostly the same meals. Boring works.
4. One relaxed meal a week is fine.
5. Water: drink until your urine is pale.

*This is general information, not medical or dietetic advice. Talk to a doctor if you have a medical condition, are on medication, or have a history of disordered eating.*
