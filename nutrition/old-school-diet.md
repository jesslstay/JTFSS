# The Old-School Diet

Old-school eating for a firm, strong body: **protein at every meal, real food, simple meals, and consistency.** No powders needed (they're fine for convenience). No crash diets.

> **Align with the book:** add the diet guidance from *Free Style Training* (meals per day, foods, how to lose fat or tone up) from `source-notes/snyder.md`, and mark it on screen as "Freestyle rule."

## Set your numbers
Starting points. Adjust every 2 weeks based on your 7-day average bodyweight, measurements and energy.

| Goal | Calories / day | Weight change target |
|---|---|---|
| Build and shape | bodyweight (lb) × 15–16 | slow gain, +0.25% of bodyweight per week |
| Maintain / recomp | bodyweight (lb) × 13–14 | stable weight, waist steady or shrinking |
| Lean out | bodyweight (lb) × 11–12 | −0.5% of bodyweight per week, at most −1% |

- **Protein:** 0.7–1 g per lb of bodyweight (or of goal weight if you have a lot to lose)
- **Fat:** at least 0.3 g per lb. Don't go very low-fat; it matters for hormones.
- **Carbs:** the rest, mostly around training
- Don't go below about 1,400 kcal without professional guidance.

*Example (140 lb, recomp):* ~1,900 kcal → 120 g protein, 55 g fat, ~230 g carbs.

## The staples
- **Protein:** eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, chicken, lean beef, fish (salmon, tuna), milk
- **Carbs:** oats, potatoes, rice, whole-grain bread, fruit, beans
- **Fats:** olive oil, nuts, avocado, cheese, a little butter
- **Vegetables:** every meal

## Template day: recomp (~1,900 kcal)
| Meal | Food |
|---|---|
| Breakfast | 2 eggs + 2 whites, 1 slice toast, berries |
| Lunch | 5 oz chicken, 1 cup rice, big salad with olive oil |
| Pre-workout | Greek yogurt with honey and fruit |
| Dinner | 5 oz salmon or lean beef, potato, vegetables |
| Evening | Cottage cheese with a few nuts |

## Template day: lean out (~1,600 kcal)
| Meal | Food |
|---|---|
| Breakfast | Greek yogurt, berries, ¼ cup oats |
| Lunch | Tuna or chicken salad, 1 slice bread |
| Snack | Apple and a string cheese |
| Dinner | 5 oz chicken or fish, vegetables, ½ cup rice or a small potato |
| Evening | Cottage cheese |

## Weekly budget grocery list
- 2 dozen eggs
- Greek yogurt and cottage cheese (2 tubs each)
- 2 lb chicken, 1 lb lean beef, 2 salmon fillets, 3 cans tuna
- Oats, rice, potatoes, whole-grain bread
- Berries (frozen is fine), apples, bananas
- Salad greens, frozen vegetables
- Olive oil, nuts, string cheese

## Rules
1. Hit protein every day. That's what keeps the "firm" while you lose fat.
2. Track your 7-day average weight **and your waist and hips**. Training can change shape while the scale barely moves.
3. Eat mostly the same meals. Boring works.
4. One relaxed meal a week is fine.
5. Water: drink until your urine is pale.

*This is general information, not medical or dietetic advice. Talk to a doctor if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have a medical condition, are on medication, or have a history of disordered eating.*
