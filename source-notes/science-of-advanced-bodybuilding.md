# Source Notes: *3 More Reps! The Science of Advanced Bodybuilding*

The channel's **second source**. *Free Style Training* (1995) is the foundation for women. This book is the **advanced, golden-era layer**: how the champions of the 1970s actually trained, and what a woman building a strong, shapely physique can borrow from them.

## What's publicly documented
- **Authors:** George Snyder and Rick Wayne. Some listings spell it "Snider."
- **Format:** a multi-volume series. Sellers list **Volume 1 (1978)** and **Volume 2 (1979)**, published by Olympus Gym-Spa. ([goldenerabookworm Vol. 1](https://www.goldenerabookworm.com/product-page/3-more-reps-volume-1), [Vol. 2](https://www.goldenerabookworm.com/product-page/3-more-reps-volume-2), [WorldCat](https://search.worldcat.org/title/Three-more-reps-:-Book-2/oclc/6805915))
- **Volume 1 champions:** Arnold Schwarzenegger, Franco Columbu, Frank Zane, Ed Corney, Robby Robinson, Mike Mentzer.
- **Volume 2 champions:** Rick Wayne, Leroy Colbert, Boyer Coe, Dave Draper, Ken Waller, Kal Szkalak, Bill Grant, Danny Padilla. Includes a short nutrition section.
- **The title:** "3 more reps" means pushing past the point where you'd normally stop. Those last reps are where the results come from.
- **Reprints:** *3 More Reps! The Science of Advanced Body Building* (2020) and *Three More Reps: The Golden Age of Bodybuilding* (2019). These are described as covering the routines of 24 champions, with added interviews (e.g. Matthias Hues, Alexander Nevsky). ([Amazon](https://us.amazon.com/Science-Advanced-Building-George-Snider/dp/B0898Z7P19), [Book Culture](https://bookculture.com/book/9781093414882))

> These details come from booksellers, not a publisher or a review, so check them against your copy. **Nothing about the routines themselves is in this repo yet. They must come from the book.**

## How the two books fit together
| | *Free Style Training* (1995) | *Science of Advanced Bodybuilding* (1978–79) |
|---|---|---|
| Audience | Women | Competitive (mostly male) bodybuilders |
| Goal | Firm and shapely, not bulky | Maximum muscle, proportion and symmetry |
| Role on the channel | **Foundation** (Weeks 1–12) | **Advanced phase** (Weeks 13–24) and the "Golden-Era Champions" series |
| Shared idea | Train to fatigue | Push for 3 more reps past it |

On camera, the line is: **"Freestyle teaches you to reach fatigue. The champions teach you what to do once you get there."**
